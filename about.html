About the application

A place to manage expenses and aim them at a goal.

Smart expense plus+ is a personal finance application for two jobs that belong together: knowing what you spend, and knowing what you are saving for.

Managing expenses

Managing expenses means writing down what you receive and what you pay, then looking at the total instead of a pile of receipts. Each transaction has a description, an amount, a date, and a category such as food, transport, bills, or health.

Income increases the balance. An expense reduces it. The category chart shows the share of spending in each group, which makes a large recurring cost visible. Filters and search help you find one payment without changing the totals. Those totals are the honest picture of what is left.

Reaching financial goals

A financial goal in this application is a named amount you want to set aside. It might be an emergency fund, a course, a trip, or the next payment on something you owe. You set the target, record what you have already saved, and add more as you put money aside.

The goal is useful only if it fits the money you actually have. Smart expense plus+ compares what your goals still need with the remaining balance from the expense tracker. If spending rises, that balance shrinks and the path to the goal gets tighter. If you spend less or record more income, you have more room to fund the goal.

How the site is organized

  • Home introduces the application and shows your latest income, expenses, balance, and goal progress.
  • Tracker is where you add, search, and remove transactions.
  • Goals is where you set a target and update what you have saved.

Where your records stay

The ledger and the goals stay in this browser on this device. Smart expense plus+ does not send them to a server. Clearing site data for this folder removes them. Open the same files in another browser and you will see an empty tracker until you enter them again.
